Emotion Slip • BG 2.47
॥ श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता ॥
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana |
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo 'stv akarmaṇi ||
सरल भावार्थ (Hindi)

तुम्हारा अधिकार केवल कर्म करने में है, उसके फलों में कभी नहीं। इसलिए कर्म के फल की इच्छा मत रखो और न ही अकर्मण्यता (कर्म न करने) के प्रति आसक्त हो।

Wisdom Translation (English)

You have a right only to perform your duty, but never to the fruits of your actions. Let not the fruits of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction.

Mindful Practice for Today

Surrender anxiety over the final outcome. Direct 100% of your current mental presence into the effort right in front of you. Peace is found in doing, not in obsessing over results.
